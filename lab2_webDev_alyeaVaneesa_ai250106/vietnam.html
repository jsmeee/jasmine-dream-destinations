Why I chose Vietnam

🍜 Food 👘 Ao dai 🏖️ Remarkable Places

Vietnam is known around the world for its fresh, flavourful street food, and its landscapes go from misty mountains to limestone bays and golden beaches. That mix is why it is first on my travel list.

Wearing the ao dai

The ao dai is Vietnam's elegant national dress, a long fitted tunic worn over loose trousers. Many tourists wear one for photos around the country.

Famous Vietnamese foods

Pho

Beef or chicken noodle soup in a slow-simmered, fragrant broth.

Soup · Noodles

Banh Mi

A crisp Vietnamese baguette sandwich packed with meat, herbs and pickles.

Street food · Sandwich

Bun Cha

Grilled meat served with rice noodles, herbs and a sweet-savoury dipping broth.

Grilled · Noodles

Spring Rolls (Goi Cuon)

Fresh rice-paper rolls filled with herbs, noodles and prawn or pork.

Fresh · Light

Bun Bo Hue

Spicy beef noodle soup from the city of Hue, bold with lemongrass and chilli.

Spicy · Soup

Banh Canh Cua

A hearty crab soup with a rich, flavorful broth and fresh ingredients.

Hearty · Soup

Banh Xeo

Crunchy rice pancake filled with shrimp, meat and herbs.

Crunchy · Pancake

Che

A sweet dessert soup with a variety of ingredients like beans, coconut milk, and fruit.

Sweet · Dessert

Ca Phe Trung

A traditional Vietnamese coffee with egg yolk, creating a rich and creamy texture.

Rich · Coffee
