Why I chose Taiwan

🍜 Food 🏖️ Remarkable Places

Taiwan is known around the world for its fresh, flavourful street food, and its landscapes go from misty mountains to limestone bays and golden beaches. That mix is why it is first on my travel list.

Famous Taiwanese foods

Lao Shan Dong

A traditional Taiwanese noodle soup with a rich, savory broth.

Soup · Noodles

Ay-Chung Rice Noodles

Delicious rice noodles served with a variety of toppings and a savory broth.

Noodles · Street food

Boba Tea

Popular Taiwanese drink with chewy tapioca pearls in a sweet tea base.

Beverage · Sweet

Crispy Milk Donut

Delicious Taiwanese snack with a crispy exterior and creamy milk filling.

Snack · Sweet

Scallion Pancake

Crispy, layered pancake filled with scallions and served with a savory dipping sauce.

Crunchy · Pancake

Xiao Long Bao

Delicate dumplings filled with soup and meat, served with a dipping sauce.

Steamer · Dumpling

Youtiao with Soy Milk

Crunchy fried dough sticks served with a warm cup of soy milk.

Crunchy · Breakfast

Oyster Omelette

A savory omelette made with fresh oysters, served with a tangy sauce.

Savory · Breakfast

Niu Rao Mian

A hearty beef noodle soup with a rich, flavorful broth and fresh ingredients.

Hearty · Soup
